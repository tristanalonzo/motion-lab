The front page. A few lines on what this whole thing is.

October 2026

thought process

This is an experiment folder like any other — the site has no special code for it. That was the point. If the front page can't live inside the same contract as everything else, the contract is too small.

All lowercase, to match the title and the list. The ↓ is drawn as a keycap rather than a bare arrow: a key tells you it's something to press, where an arrow just points. It's a picture of a key, not a button, so the pill-or-circle rule doesn't apply to it.

inspirations

  • Apple's quiet first screens — one sentence, then the way in.
  • A good README's opening line, which tells you what something is before it tells you anything else.

what could come next
